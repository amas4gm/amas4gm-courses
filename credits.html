References and model credits

Bambu Studio reference

This AMAS4GM learning simulation recreates the Home, Prepare, Preview, and Device workspaces from screenshots of the actual installed Bambu Studio application, captured on November 5–6, 2026. It is not Bambu Studio, is not affiliated with Bambu Lab, and cannot slice models or send print jobs. Controls outside the current learning step are disabled.

Navigation follows the default left-drag orbit, right/middle-drag pan, and wheel zoom behavior. Dragging to orbit starts on empty space. Your installed version or personal settings may differ. The reset button and accessible practice aids are tutorial controls, not representations of Bambu Studio controls.

Bambu Studio viewport source · Download Bambu Studio

The Move, Scale, Rotate, and Lay on face panels use the installed application as a visual reference. The object tools are a teaching simulation with a single model and a subset of the real software’s features. Bambu Studio Lay on face source · Bambu Studio rotation handles and snap bands source.

Physical lab simulation

Tutorial 03 represents the AMAS4GM lab with two named X2D printers, a shared workstation, and simplified AMS 2 Pro controls. It covers physical printer selection and filament loading, ending at filament intake.

Slicing and calculator practice

Tutorial 04 uses estimates checked in the installed Bambu Studio Benchy project on November 6, 2026: X2D, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.08 mm High Quality. Without supports: 11.80 g, $0.24 material cost, 1h40m. With automatic tree supports: 12.72 g, $0.25, 1h50m. Layer previews, support shapes, and printing are simplified simulations, not calculated toolpaths. The price check uses current rates from the AMAS calculator and its calculation module. Calculator practice does not take a payment or require printing a bill.

Benchy practice model

The tutorial loads the exact Benchy.stl supplied with this course project. Tutorial 01 preserves its imported sideways orientation. Tutorial 02 lets learners change its position, scale, and orientation without modifying the original STL file.
