Bambu Studio reference

This AMAS4GM learning simulation recreates the Home, Prepare, Preview, and Device workspaces from screenshots of the actual installed Bambu Studio application, captured on November 5–6, 2026. It is not Bambu Studio, is not affiliated with Bambu Lab, and cannot slice models or send print jobs. Controls outside the current learning step are disabled.

Navigation follows the default left-drag orbit, right/middle-drag pan, and wheel zoom behavior. Dragging to orbit starts on empty space. Your installed version or personal settings may differ. Touchpads can use primary-click drag to orbit, secondary-click drag to pan, and two-finger scrolling to zoom. On touchscreens, the simulator uses one-finger drag to orbit, two-finger slide to pan, and pinch/spread to zoom. Native gesture availability and scrolling direction depend on the operating system and settings. Optional object-tool practice aids are tutorial controls, not representations of Bambu Studio controls.

Bambu Studio viewport source · Download Bambu Studio

The Move, Scale, Rotate, and Lay on face panels use the installed application as a visual reference. The object tools are a teaching simulation with a single model and a subset of the real software’s features. Bambu Studio Lay on face source · Bambu Studio rotation handles and snap bands source.

Slicing and calculator practice

Tutorial 04 uses estimates checked in the installed Bambu Studio Benchy project on November 6, 2026: X2D, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.08 mm High Quality. Without supports: 11.80 g, $0.24 material cost, 1h40m. With automatic tree supports: 12.72 g, $0.25, 1h50m. Walls, infill, surfaces, bridges, and support paths were extracted from the supplied Benchy project sliced in Bambu Studio 02.08.02.61 on November 9, 2026, including the branches through the doorway to the roof underside. The layer slider reveals those real deposited paths, including the spaces between walls and infill. Prepare uses the original model surface. Extrusion cross-sections are simplified for browser rendering; printer motion and elapsed time are illustrative. The price check uses current rates from the AMAS calculator and its calculation module. Calculator practice does not take a payment or require printing a bill.
